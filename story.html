Spotlight 1: Instruments

All That Glitters: The Story of Tan’s Sparkle Finishes

In the heart of a small town, a young guitar maker named Tan started a quiet revolution, one sparkle at a time. Known at first only in local music circles, Tan wasn’t just building guitars; he was making dreams that shined. His trademark? Dazzling sparkle finishes that didn’t just look good, they told a story.

What began as an experiment using leftover materials turned into a signature style. Tan’s first glitter guitar was a gift for a friend who had just landed his first gig. That night, under the lights, the guitar sparkled like a star, and the crowd noticed. Word spread quickly. Suddenly, everyone wanted “that sparkle guitar.”

Each design was different. Some sparkled like stars, others glowed like neon lights at night. Every guitar that came out of Tan’s small workshop was hand-painted with care, coated layer by layer, until the finish shimmered in the light. For Tan, it wasn’t about being perfect, it was about being unique.

As Tan’s name grew in the music scene, even international artists started to notice. A well-known jazz guitarist visiting the Philippines saw one of Tan’s guitars at a local gig and immediately placed an order. That moment marked Tan’s first step onto the global stage. His designs were no longer just a local secret, they were becoming known worldwide.

But Tan stayed humble. Despite the fame, he never forgot his roots. He started offering affordable sparkle finishes for young, struggling musicians who couldn’t afford expensive custom work. He believed that every artist deserved a guitar that reflected their inner shine, no matter their status or income.

He also released a limited-edition series called “Legends in Sparkle,” featuring tribute designs inspired by famous guitarists. Each piece told a different story, from classic rock stars to local street performers who shaped the sound of underground Manila. Fans and collectors lined up just to get one.

And while his glitter finishes were bold, Tan himself remained low-key. He preferred to let his work speak for him. His message was clear: “Every strum, every solo, every note deserves to shine.”

As years went by, Tan’s sparkle finishes became more than a trend, they became a movement. They reminded musicians to embrace boldness, express their individuality, and never settle for ordinary. Whether you’re a beginner or a pro, holding one of Tan’s guitars feels like you’re holding a piece of your own story.

Because in the end, it wasn’t just about glitter. It was about turning every guitar into a dream that shines.

Spotlight 2: Parts and Accessories

The right accessories are crucial to looking and sounding your best, whether you’re sitting down to strum your first chords or getting ready for your next big gig. And while picking out new gear to pair your guitar or bass with is always going to be a part of your musical journey, we’ve got five must-haves that you need to hit the ground running.

A CABLE

This is an absolute requirement for any electric guitar or bass player. It’s the direct line between your instrument and your amp, so you’re going to want one that you can rely on every time you plug in. There are dozens of options available — including different colors, lengths, materials and connector types. There’s even artist signature models if you want to be inspired by your favorite performers.

A STRAP

If you have any ambition to play while standing up you’re going to need a strap. They’re vital for everything from strumming on an acoustic to doing rock ‘n’ roll windmills on an electric. With a variety of colors, thicknesses, designs and materials to choose from, there’s a perfect one out there to suit your specific needs and show off your personal style.

PICKS

Every player — acoustic or electric, guitar or bass — should always have some picks in their case. They’re great for a variety of styles and genres, plus they help improve your playing precision and volume. They come in a wide selection of shapes and thicknesses and it’s good to try a few out until you find the ideal one. And even if you’re committed to fingerpicking or plucking, it’s always good to have picks on hand to share with your bandmates and fellow players who forgot theirs.

A GIG BAG

One of the best ways to protect your guitar or bass when you’re traveling or storing it is with a gig bag. They’re lightweight and come in different sizes to fit any instrument. Select from a range of padding options, colors, patterns and materials — including basic black, denim, checkerboard or even Hello Kitty pink!

A STAND

Stands make it easy to showcase, protect and reach for your instrument at home, on stage or in the studio. And with a wide selection of choices — including adjustable length, multi-guitar, stylish wood and more — there’s a great option for every collection and space. Check out our new hanging and cradle stands for the ideal combination of stability and style.
